Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 August 2026

RISE Roofing Solutions is a roofing company, not a data company. The only information we ever get from this website is what you choose to type into the contact form. This page explains exactly what happens to it, in plain English.

The short version

  • The contact form is the only thing that collects anything.
  • What you send comes to RISE Roofing Solutions.
  • We never sell it, and never share it for anyone else's marketing.
  • This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking.
  • Want your information deleted? Email or call and ask. We'll do it.

What the contact form collects

Exactly these four things, and nothing else:

  • Your name — required
  • Your phone number — required
  • Your email address — optional
  • Your message, if you write one — optional

The form also quietly records which page you submitted it from — the Tupelo page, the storm damage page, and so on. That is only so we know what you were reading when you got in touch.

The form does not ask for your address. If you tell us where the house is in the message box, that is up to you, and it is treated the same way as everything else here.

Where it goes

Submissions come to RISE Roofing Solutions, and are seen by the members of our team who handle your inquiry — the people who call you back, price the work, or book the inspection.

There is no marketing platform, no CRM, and no lead-selling service attached to this form.

What we use it for

To answer you. That means calling or emailing you back, giving you a quote, booking an inspection, or answering whatever you asked.

We will not add you to a mailing list or send you marketing because you filled in the form. If you want to hear from us about something else, you have to ask.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing. There are three situations where it goes beyond our own team:

  • The service providers that run this website for us — see the next section.
  • If we're handling an insurance claim for you — see below.
  • If we were legally required to hand it over — a court order, for example.

Service providers

We use third-party services to operate this website and process form submissions. Our hosting and form handling come from Netlify, so what you send is stored on Netlify's servers as well as reaching us.

These providers hold your information only in order to provide that service to us, and not for any purpose of their own.

If we're handling your insurance claim

If you hire us for storm damage work and we work the claim with you, we share what the claim needs with your insurance company and the adjuster assigned to it. In practice that means your name and the property address, our photographs of the damage, and our written scope of work.

That is the whole point of us being involved — the insurer can't assess a claim it has no documentation for. But it only ever happens for a claim you have asked us to work on. Filling in the contact form does not send anything to an insurer, and we don't contact one on your behalf without you asking us to.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies at all. There is no Google Analytics or any other analytics, no advertising or tracking pixels, and nothing that follows you to other sites. The video on the front page is a file hosted on this site, not a YouTube or Vimeo embed, so no third party sees that you watched it.

The one unavoidable thing: like every website, ours runs on a server, and Netlify keeps ordinary server logs — roughly, which page was requested, when, and the IP address it was requested from. That is standard for any site on the internet and we don't use those logs for anything.

We use a hidden "honeypot" field to catch spam bots, rather than a reCAPTCHA. It is invisible to you, requires nothing from you, and involves no third party.

How to have your information deleted

Email us at david.knight@riseroofingms.com or call (662) 213-4797, and tell us you want your information deleted. We'll remove it from our own records and from the Netlify account, and confirm once it's done.

You can also just ask what we're holding and we'll tell you. You don't need to give a reason for either request.

Children

This site is for homeowners arranging roofing work. It isn't aimed at children, and we don't knowingly collect anything from them.

If this policy changes

If we ever add anything to the site that collects more than the form does — analytics, for instance — we'll update this page and change the date at the top.
