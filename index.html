Meet the founder

David Knight

Founder & Owner, RISE Roofing Solutions

Born and raised in Mantachie, MS, David Knight founded RISE Roofing Solutions to bring honesty, transparency, and lasting quality back to the roofing industry. Before entering the trade six years ago, David served as a youth leader in Houston, MS — an experience that cemented his core belief in leading with integrity and earning people's trust.

A proud family man, David runs RISE Roofing Solutions on simple, non-negotiable values: straight answers instead of sales pressure, pricing you can actually understand, and craftsmanship that stands the test of time.
